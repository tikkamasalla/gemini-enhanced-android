Gemini Enhanced is an unofficial Android app for Google Gemini. It shows the Gemini web app in a WebView and adds a native chat sidebar, replies that finish after you leave the app, and a strict allow list for network requests.

Features:
* Native sidebar with your chats, opened with a swipe from anywhere or with the menu button. It follows your finger, shows pinned chats and the open chat, and appears at once on start from a local cache.
* Replies keep streaming when you switch apps, turn the screen off or swipe the app away. A notification shows while Gemini is replying and tells you when the reply is ready.
* Opens the chat you had open last.
* No blank or black screen when you come back to the app. If the page crashes, it is rebuilt with your chat.
* Near zero CPU use while idle.
* Code and HTML previews work, including the chart and script libraries they load.
* Saves generated images to Downloads.
* Phones get the mobile layout, tablets the desktop layout.

Privacy:
* Only Google hosts that Gemini needs are allowed. Every other request is blocked, and plain HTTP is never loaded.
* Links to other sites open in your browser.
* No analytics, no ads, no trackers. WebView usage reporting is off and app backup is off.

This app is not affiliated with Google. You need a Google account to use Gemini.
